FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things founders ask us most.

What is StartupSense AI?

StartupSense AI is an AI-powered web app that analyzes your startup idea based on details like industry, business model, target customers, budget and competition, then gives you an easy-to-understand evaluation.

Who is it for?

It is designed for students, first-time founders and anyone exploring a business idea who wants a quick, structured way to assess its potential before investing resources.

What information do I need to provide?

Your startup name, a description of your idea, industry, business model, target customers, estimated initial budget and a rating of how competitive the market is.

Is my data secure?

You create an account and log in to access your own dashboard. Account creation and login verification are handled by a dedicated database module, and you can change your password anytime in Settings.

Can I see my previous analyses?

Yes. The Analysis History section lets you view analyses generated earlier during your session.

Does the AI guarantee my startup will succeed?

No. The analysis is a decision-support tool that helps you think through your idea. It is not a guarantee of success, so always combine it with your own market research and judgment.
